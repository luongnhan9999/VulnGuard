=== DEFI VAULT SECURITY BOUNTY POLICY ===

1. SCOPE & IN-SCOPE ASSETS
- Smart contract vault logic at 0x1234567890abcdef1234567890abcdef12345678
- Reentrancy vulnerabilities, arithmetic overflows/underflows, improper access control, precision loss.

2. SEVERITY TIERS
- CRITICAL: Direct theft of user funds, permanent freezing of assets, flashloan exploit leading to pool drain.
- MEDIUM: Partial loss of yield, temporary state manipulation, unhandled edge cases causing localized failure.

3. OUT-OF-SCOPE & INVALID SUBMISSIONS
- Theoretical attacks without practical proof or executable exploit steps.
- Social engineering, phishing, physical security, volumetric DDoS attacks.
- Third-party dependency issues outside our smart contract core logic.

4. REWARD STRUCTURE & ANTI-SPAM
- Critical Severity: 100% of available program bounty pool + 100% bond refund.
- Medium Severity: 50% of available program bounty pool + 100% bond refund.
- Invalid Submission: Bond refunded, 0 bounty payout.
- Spam / Malicious Submission: Bond slashed and transferred to program owner.
